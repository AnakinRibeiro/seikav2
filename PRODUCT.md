## Users

- Residential owners with high energy bills who want to cut costs and are evaluating solar.
- Businesses and institutions: industries, commerce, rural producers, supermarkets, condominiums. They need cost predictability, technical compliance, and minimal disruption to operations.
- Secondary: companies seeking electrical projects, ICMS recovery studies, technical reports (laudos) and consulting.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for SEIKA Engenharia, an electrical engineering and solar energy company. Success = the visitor sends a photo of their energy bill via WhatsApp (primary conversion) or explores the services.

## Positioning

"Uma empresa de engenharia, não uma revenda de painéis." Every project is sized from the client's real consumption, under the technical responsibility of engenheiro Adriano José Gorges (CREA-PR 206130/D). One technical owner for the full cycle: feasibility, project, execution, homologation, and generation follow-up.

## Product Principles

1. Engineering credibility over sales pitch: show the responsible engineer, norms, and process.
2. Lowest-friction conversion: one photo of the bill on WhatsApp, no commitment, no cost.
3. Real work as proof: the client's own installations carry the gallery.
4. Serve both homes and businesses without diluting either.
