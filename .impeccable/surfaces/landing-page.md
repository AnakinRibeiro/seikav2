# Surface: Landing page Seika (Figma, Desktop 1440)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: homeowners with high bills and businesses (industry, commerce, rural, condos). Action: send energy bill photo via WhatsApp / simulate savings. Proof: responsible engineer + CREA, 12 real install photos, 3 real testimonials (stars, no numeric rating). Constraints: Inter only, max 64px (hero h1), body 16px; Spacing + Radius variables on every padding/gap/radius; Desktop 12-col grid (80/16); brand paint styles Primary (amber) and Secondary (blue).

Deliverable: two full Desktop options (A, B) in Figma page "Landing Page", each testing a different reference approach per section; the user will pick per section for a refined final.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Engineering firm, not a panel reseller: photography of sun on real roofs carries the page; refuses the generic green-eco solar template.
OWN-WORLD: Deep secondary-900 navy fields, amber (primary-500) as the single action/sun colour, white and secondary-50 grounds; Inter in tight display weights; hairline rules; glass only where the brief asks (hero badge, hero review card).
STORY: Visitor sees solar is real and local, believes an engineer owns the project end to end, sends the bill on WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo, gradient overlay, H1 56px + subtitle + two CTAs anchored at bottom; glass badge above H1; A adds a glass review card bottom-right.
FORM: Pinned by the user's reference board and comments. seed: none (pinned) — "Background em destaque com um leve overlay... H1 e botões na parte de baixo da hero... Elemento em cima do H1 com efeito de transparência e blur".
